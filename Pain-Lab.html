Part 1: Pain Dial Simulator

Teaching modelThis is a simplified picture of how inputs turn pain up or down. The meter is a teaching model, not a measurement, and its numbers are not real units. Real people vary a great deal. It is not a tool for assessing or treating anyone.

MeasureValue

How the model works: tissue input sets nociceptor traffic. Poor sleep, ongoing stress and fear raise a "sensitivity" level that amplifies spinal transmission and can add signal even with no tissue input, like nociplastic pain. Touch and movement send A beta input that partly closes the spinal gate. Movement, expecting relief and sudden danger strengthen the descending brakes; stress, fear, expecting harm and poor sleep strengthen the boosters. Fear, stress and expectation also change how threatening the brain judges the signal. All weights are invented for teaching.
