MeasureValue

Simplified teaching model with illustrative values. Joint gap is drawn from force above the preload (about 0.0015 mm per newton, exaggerated in the picture). Sensor firing rises with how fast force climbs, a pattern taken from animal studies. Cavitation appears when the rate of force is at least 1,500 N/s and the force climbs at least 150 N; that is a made up teaching rule, not a measured threshold. Real tissues, people and techniques vary a lot.
